Pdf Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Pdf Reader ("the app") is a PDF reader, scanner and editor for Android, published on Google Play by its developer ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Your documents stay on your phone

Everything the app does with your files happens on your device: opening, reading, searching, scanning, compressing, merging, splitting, signing and editing. Your documents, scans and signatures are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. A file leaves your phone only when you choose to share it (for example to WhatsApp or email) through Android's share sheet.

If you grant "All files access", the app uses it only to find documents on your phone and show them in your library. The list of your files stays on your device.

Information collected by services in the app

The app is free and is supported by ads and optional purchases. These services, run by other companies, receive some information to work:

Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, never inside the reader, editor, scanner or paywall. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and European Economic Area), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases — Google Play and RevenueCat

If you buy "Remove ads" or Pro, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat processes your purchase records (what you bought, when, and whether it is active) so the app can unlock what you paid for and restore it later. RevenueCat identifies you only by a random ID created by the app, not by your name or email, and the app does not send it your advertising ID.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Crash reports — Sentry

When the app hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry so we can fix it. Reports contain technical details (error message, app version, phone model, Android version). Before a report is sent, the app removes file names, file paths and other personal details, and it never includes the contents of your documents.

Sentry's privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

App settings — Firebase Remote Config

The app downloads settings from Google Firebase Remote Config (for example how often ads may appear). To do this, Firebase creates an installation ID for the app on your phone.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Usage statistics — Firebase Analytics

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics, but it is switched off and collects nothing in the current version. If we turn it on in a future version, we will update this policy first, tell you in the app, and give you a switch in Settings to turn it off. It will never receive file names, file paths or document contents.

Document scanner — Google ML Kit

The document scanner uses Google ML Kit, provided by Google Play services on your phone. Google Play services may download the scanner the first time you use it, and ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical usage information about the scanner. Your scanned images are not sent.

App lock and reviews

If you turn on App lock, your fingerprint, face or phone PIN is checked by Android itself; the app never receives or stores them. If the app asks you to rate it, the rating screen is shown by the Google Play Store app, and your review goes to Google Play under its policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not upload your documents, scans or signatures.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera roll, microphone or SMS. The camera is used only by the document scanner, when you open it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone (your library, settings and app data) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Information held by the services above is kept under their own policies. You can ask us to help with a request about information connected to the app by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
